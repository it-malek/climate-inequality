# Historical research evidence

Five byte-preserved contracts and cited records are indexed in
[`evidence/manifest.json`](evidence/manifest.json), with original paths,
SHA-256 digests and the preserved source commit. Their original relative
references describe the historical checkout and may not resolve in this snapshot.
They record scientific execution constraints; they are not instructions to rerun a study.

Use [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) for final interpretations,
[`VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md`](VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md) for methodology and
[`canonical_results.json`](../../reports/canonical_results.json) for the
FINAL / SUPERSEDED / HISTORICAL source index. The index references source values
rather than maintaining a second set of estimates.

Verify retained evidence with `python -m src.research_audit`.
